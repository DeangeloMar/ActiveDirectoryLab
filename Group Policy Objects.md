<h1>Password Policy</h1>

<h2>Description</h2>
This guide walks through creating and configuring a password policy for a domain using Group Policy Management.
<br />

<h2>walk-through</h2>
<p align="center">
Click Search in the bottom-left corner of the screen: <br/>
<img width="1917" height="1038" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/57b24893-57f9-4930-8397-6f98e3a7aee8" />
<br />
<br />
Search for Group Policy Management and select it to launch the utility:  <br/>
<img width="1919" height="1036" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b7c99a85-0a59-4ce5-8e94-50045173600f" />
<br />
<br />
Under Domains, right-click your domain and select Create a GPO in this domain, and link it here...: <br/>
<img width="1918" height="1034" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7609aa6-1ca1-4441-9d60-181a51b319b7" />
<br />
<br />
Name the policy Password Policy and click OK:  <br/>
<img width="1919" height="1040" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d85dab0d-5eb5-4aee-a8ac-15aa248050ce" />
<br />
<br />
Right-click the new policy and select Edit:  <br/>
<img width="1919" height="1034" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/078da878-fc71-4e69-9e4f-9f38ecf23a2a" />
<br />
<br />
In the Group Policy Management Editor, go to Computer Configuration > Policies > Windows Settings > Security Settings > Account Policies > Password Policy. Configure the settings you want (minimum length, complexity, maximum age, etc.):  <br/>
<img width="1919" height="1036" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fc693598-ce3a-41a3-94e5-1e1b56907d3d" />

<br />
Verify the new password requirements are enforced:  <br/>
